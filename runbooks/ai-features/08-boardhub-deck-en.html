Capturing the meeting

The meeting writes its own minutes

Recording runs in the room, on the browser — not inside your conferencing vendor. That is why it works the same whichever platform the meeting is on.

Any meeting platformIt records the room microphone and the meeting audio in the browser tab, so Teams, Webex, Zoom or Meet all work — with no integration and no bot joining the call.
Bilingual minutes, in one passArabic and English minutes are drafted together, with action items, owners and due dates. It will not invent an attendee, a decision or a deadline, and nothing is filed until someone approves it.
One conversation, one ownerEach assistant thread belongs to a single user; nobody else in the organisation can open it. Enforced in the code, not by convention.
The recording is never keptAudio is transcribed and discarded — the platform stores no recording. For sensitive sessions, transcription can run fully on-device instead of through a cloud model.

Models and deployment

Your models. Your cloud.
Your data boundary.

The platform is model-agnostic by design: every provider routes through one abstraction, so the model is an administrative choice rather than an architectural commitment.

Foundation models

AWS BedrockPrimary. Chat, streaming, tool-calling and embeddings, inside your AWS account.
AWS Bedrock AgentCoreThe agent is built on AWS's open-source Strands framework, the same runtime AgentCore hosts.
IBM watsonxSupported for chat and embeddings; tool-calling varies by model and is validated per model.
Alibaba Cloud (Qwen)Already in production here — Arabic retrieval runs on Qwen3 embeddings today.
Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouterDirect or routed access when a specific model is preferred.
Ollama — fully offlineOpen-weight models on your own hardware, with no outbound call at all.
